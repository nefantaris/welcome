Posts are markdown files in `content/posts/`. Each one needs a title and a
date in its frontmatter: the date orders the blog index and shows on the post
itself. A post with `draft: true` stays out of the build until you remove it.

This post exists so the blog index and the home page have something to show.
Replace it, or write the next one beside it and it will appear above this one.
